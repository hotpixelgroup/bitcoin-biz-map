# Great Lakes and Kentucky merchant research

Checked 2026-10-20. Searched every assigned state: Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, Kentucky. This primary-source sample is not exhaustive. It supplements root's national BTC Map collection rather than repackaging OSM mirrors as independent confirmation. No outreach or payments were made.

`great-lakes-primary.json` contains 17 records: 10 merchant-confirmed, 6 needs-review, and 1 excluded. Confirmation means a current public merchant statement was read, not a live payment test. Explicit network support is recorded only when stated. False network values mean unknown unless the summary explicitly says otherwise. Coordinates were not guessed; match official addresses to national BTC Map records or geocode with attribution. `existing: null` means the existing catalog was not checked by this research agent.

## Material limits and negative evidence

- [Mycolabs official FAQ](https://www.mycolabs.com/store/pages/44/FAQ): explicitly says it does not accept Bitcoin currently. Saved `excluded`. Do not confuse its FAQ page title mentioning Midwest Grow Kits with the separate wholesaler; exclusion applies to Mycolabs.
- [Bloombrite Bitcoin URL](https://www.bloombritecleaning.com/bitcoin-payments/) currently produces a generic house cleaning page without the indexed BTC acceptance text. This is not an explicit refusal; retained needs-review.
- [Wolfe Legal Services](https://ohiolaw.net/frequently-asked-questions/do-attorneys-accept-bitcoin/) excludes expenses from BTC payments and may charge fees. Preserve those limits.
- [Winewood Organics](https://www.winewoodorganics.com/faq) accepts BTC over Lightning at physical pickup. It is an age-restricted cannabis retailer; do not infer medical claims from accepting payment.
- [Legacy Auctions current sale terms](https://hibid.com/illinois/auction/780890/fine-art--exquisite-collectibles--and-southwest-jewelry) confirm BTC and a permanent pickup facility, but visits are scheduled, purchase terms may change, and this particular auction closes October 25, 2026. Do not advertise unrestricted walk-in retail.
- [Half A Pond homepage](https://halfapond.com/) currently gives the Hawesville shop street address and variable-hours notice; [shop page](https://halfapond.com/hotmess/) gives the Bitcoin policy and local pickup. Direct fetch of the shop page returned an older cached variant mentioning appointment visits and PO Box; use the current homepage street address with contact-before-visiting copy.
- [Heartland contact page](https://www.heartlandmedianetworks.com/contact.html) direct fetch failed, but its search index returned the full official acceptance policy, address and hours. Policy has a 2015 effective date while official homepage is still operating; confirm invoice arrangements first.

## Other leads not promoted to verified physical merchants

- **Old Major Market, IN:** [official homepage](https://oldmajormarket.com/) confirms 4201 Millersville Rd Suite 119, Indianapolis and local pickup; recent Bitcoin Magazine reporting says it accepts BTC, but no direct merchant payment statement was found on the pages checked. Root may have this already in BTC Map.
- **The Rejoicing Vine, IN:** recent reporting and BTC Map mirror has Bitcoin acceptance; direct website was inaccessible in this pass. No independent merchant confirmation asserted.
- **Overhauser Law Offices, IN:** direct current office contact page exists, but the acceptance stories surfaced were from 2014/2015. The 2026 Bitcoin Magazine Indiana appendix is a useful community lead, not proof from the merchant.
- **Noblesville Imports, IN:** 2021 Reddit claim led to current dealership site but no current explicit BTC acceptance statement found. Not promoted.
- **Switchyard Brewing, IN:** historical 2022 employee post surfaced. No current merchant-owned evidence verified; not promoted.
- **Fork & Coin, IL:** [primary chef/co-founder interview](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-bitcoin-supper-club-fork-coin/id1794318447?i=1000767193739) and [current community event address](https://www.meetup.com/bitcoin-open-blockchain-community-chicago/events/gxwjbvyjcnbjb/?eventOrigin=group_upcoming_events). Merchant domain fetch failed; map dataset likely already contains it.
- **Revive Dental:** surfaced for a Michigan query because of an insurance name, but the [official payment page](https://revivedent.com/payment-options/) clearly locates it at 8333 W McNab Rd #104, Tamarac, **Florida**. It accepts BTC with 5% discount, but must not be misassigned to MI.
- **Arotags and Coin Vigilante, OH:** direct BTC statements found, but no public visitor retail location established; online shipping business alone does not warrant a physical pin.
- **Grayshark, West Side Lawn Care and similar providers:** service-area BTC acceptance is real, but a town centroid, arbitrary cafe or residential address should not be presented as an open storefront.

ATM/exchange services, donation-only organizations and vague crypto-only statements were excluded from confirmation. Keyword search results alone were followed to the merchant wherever possible; exact evidence URLs and conditions remain in JSON for independent review.
