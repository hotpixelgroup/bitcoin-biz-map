# Mid-Atlantic primary-source research

Checked October 20, 2026. Region: West Virginia, Pennsylvania, Maryland, Delaware, Washington DC, New Jersey. This is a targeted independent corroboration pass, not an exhaustive census. Root separately imports national BTC Map records; OSM-derived directories were used as leads rather than independent confirmation. No businesses were contacted and no payment was tested.

The companion JSON contains 16 records: nine merchant-confirmed locations, six needs-review leads, and one exclusion for map suitability. Two confirmations (Daisy's Deli and PubKey DC) already appear in the current national dataset. No coordinates were guessed. Unknown payment rails are null. Dates describe the actual evidence, not a fresh in-person payment test.

## Pennsylvania

- [Twin Pine Ford purchase preparation page](https://www.twinpineford.com/what-to-bring.html) explicitly accepts Bitcoin with a **5% processing fee**, at 620 N Reading Road, Ephrata PA 17522. Live direct merchant policy; no date on policy.
- [McKenzie Law Firm's Bitcoin FAQ](https://www.davidmckenzielawfirm.com/faqs/does-mckenzie-law-firm-accept-bitcoin-as-payment/) explicitly accepts BTC for legal services. Its [contact page](https://www.davidmckenzielawfirm.com/contact-us/) labels three physical/mailing offices and publishes visitor hours: Blue Bell, Exton and King of Prussia. The firmwide policy supports these offices; do not infer additional practice-area SEO pages are offices.
- [Hardwood Flooring by Christopher](https://www.floorphilly.com/) directly offers Bitcoin payment discounts, but no public visitor street address was established. It describes in-home consultations and Skippack PA 19426; held for review rather than pinning a town centroid.
- Searches included Pennsylvania Bitcoin merchants, Philadelphia flooring/payment policies, Ephrata vehicle purchases and the law firm's own payment and contact pages. State coverage is not exhaustive.

## Sheetz / Flexa caveat across the region

[Flexa's original May 2021 Sheetz announcement](https://medium.com/flexa/sheetz-becomes-first-convenience-store-chain-to-accept-bitcoin-1e4ae8649854) described enabling payments, with initial pilots and planned rollout. Later 2024 announcements described broader in-store expansion. These do not on their own refresh every branch's October 2026 payment instructions.

The provider's [SPEDN shutdown FAQ](https://support.flexa.co/en/articles/13569029-faqs-regarding-the-spedn-app-shutdown) says **SPEDN closed March 31, 2026**. Old instructions to install/use SPEDN are therefore stale. Flexa itself continues through wallet/SDK integrations; app shutdown is not evidence that all Flexa merchants refuse Bitcoin. Reconfirm a current supported payment route before importing entire chains or publishing app instructions. No fuel-at-pump acceptance was inferred.
