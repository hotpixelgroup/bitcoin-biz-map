# Southwest merchant research — checked October 20, 2026

Scope: Arizona, New Mexico, Nevada, Utah, Colorado and Wyoming. This is a targeted primary-source corroboration pass, not a complete census. No business was contacted and no purchase was made. A published merchant statement confirms the merchant's stated policy; it does not demonstrate that a particular cashier can complete a transaction today. Unknown Bitcoin rails remain `null` instead of being inferred from a processor brand.

The companion JSON contains 22 records: 14 `merchant-confirmed`, six `needs-review`, and two explicit exclusions. Coordinates were not invented or inferred from city centers. Candidate physical locations still need sourced coordinates before publication.

## Arizona omissions

Seven physical offices have strong published merchant evidence and are absent from the original 219-name directory:

- **Palm Valley Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics:** six current offices, at Scottsdale (21070 N Pima Rd), Goodyear (14555 W Indian School Rd #420), Buckeye (20755 W McDowell Rd #100), Surprise (14843 W Bell Rd #100), Chandler (920 E Riggs Rd), and East Mesa (4425 S Mountain Rd #109). The practice-owned [Bitcoin policy page](https://pvpd.com/pvpd-accepts-bitcoin-and-crypto-for-dental-payments-in-arizona/) explicitly offers Bitcoin for dental and orthodontic services and explains QR-code payment. The JSON links each branch's official address page. This is a practice-wide policy, not six independently tested transactions. No specific rail is stated.
- **Live To Smile / George Carr DDS & Associates**, 2072 E Southern Ave A103, Tempe. Its current [payment options page](https://www.livetosmile.com/payment-options) explicitly offers Bitcoin, requests advance coordination and publishes its location on the same page. This is stronger than relying solely on the widely syndicated 2018 launch story. No specific rail is stated.

Additional Arizona leads deliberately held for review:

- [Oatman Farms Bitcoin page](https://oatmanfarms.com/pages/bitcoin) offers Bitcoin for online provisions, but its [contact page](https://oatmanfarms.com/pages/contact-us) explicitly says the farm is not open to the public. A PO Box is not a visitor map pin. Market appearances should be separately verified for dates and Bitcoin checkout.
- [A1 Garage Door Service's announcement](https://a1garage.com/a1-garage-door-service-accepts-first-bitcoin-payment/) is dated August 31, 2021. It describes a real completed Bitcoin purchase, but does not establish current acceptance at every branch. Do not automatically multiply it across the company's national offices.
- [ITM Trading FAQ](https://www.itmtrading.com/faq) currently offers Bitcoin through BitPay for precious-metal orders, but the address is given for mailing payments. Walk-in retail access at the Phoenix office is unconfirmed.
- [Avnet's March 2019 release](https://news.avnet.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2019/Avnet-Leads-Industry-by-Accepting-Cryptocurrency/default.aspx) is actual merchant evidence for B2B Bitcoin purchases. Excluded from candidate JSON because this pass did not establish current policy or a customer-facing physical location. An office headquarters alone would not justify a directions pin.
- Arizona Sedan and Limousine Service's [March 25, 2025 post](https://arizonasedanandlimo.net/blog-arizona-sedan-and-limousine-service-to-accept-bitcoin-payments-starting-in-2025.html) describes a future launch and repeatedly says “coming soon.” It does not prove launch occurred; not included as a verified merchant.

Existing Pita Cafe, Scottsdale Burger Bar and Echo Coffee were corroborated by the [October 19, 2026 source article](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/culture/pita-cafe-arizona-bitcoin). No duplicate candidate records were made.

## New Mexico

Lightning instructions specifically cover the online guest checkout. In-store rail not independently tested.

The older automatic-checkout instructions farther down should not be treated as current. Hold for review as an advance-arranged purchase rather than promise walk-in Bitcoin checkout.

Searches covered Albuquerque and Santa Fe along with statewide merchant-payment queries. Bitcoin ATM pages were excluded.

## Nevada

- **Golden Gate Hotel & Casino** and **The D Las Vegas**: the hotel operator's [Bitcoin article](https://www.goldengatecasino.com/blog/best-ways-utilize-bitcoin-vegas/) explicitly offers BitPay for hotel/dining/gift-shop purchases and explicitly excludes gambling. This is historical content still published by the operator; a recent transaction was not tested. Current official address pages are linked in JSON. Do not generalize it to Circa or to casino-floor wagers.
- **Cane Juice Bar & Café**, 7925 S Rainbow Blvd: [January 2026 FOX5 interview](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/01/23/las-vegas-businesses-accept-bitcoin-payments-cryptocurrency-gains-mainstream-adoption/) directly quotes its district manager confirming Bitcoin. Its [merchant Square storefront](https://canejuicebarcafe.square.site/) provides the matching address. Held as corroborated review lead because a merchant-owned Bitcoin statement was not found.
- Crazy Horse 3 has a [merchant-issued July 2021 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crazy-horse-3-makes-history-as-first-major-entertainment-venue-in-las-vegas-to-accept-bitcoin-301331122.html) for online VIP packages via Lightning, but this is old, limited-scope evidence. It was not added to the candidate set as a current in-person payment location.

Searches covered Las Vegas, statewide physical retailers, dentists and hospitality. Hosting an ATM was not accepted as proof of Bitcoin merchant payment.

## Colorado

- **Sheridan Billiards / Colorado Pool Tables**: its [merchant Bitcoin page](https://www.coloradopooltables.com/bitcoin) explicitly accepts Bitcoin; the text dates itself to September 2021. Its [current homepage](https://www.coloradopooltables.com/) gives showroom address 2653 W Hampden Ave, Sheridan. Keep the old evidence date visible.
- **Denver Gutter Company**: [merchant post](https://www.denverguttercompany.com/blog/why-we-accept-bitcoin) explicitly offers Bitcoin for gutters. Exact Englewood office address comes from the [local chamber](https://web.bestchamber.com/Home-Care/Denver-Gutter-Company-27127), while the [merchant contact page](https://www.denverguttercompany.com/contact) confirms its service base. Physical walk-in access remains unconfirmed, so hold for review.

Two important negative observations override unqualified directory imports:

- **Fifth House Coffee**: [firsthand October 13, 2026 report](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/culture/moonflower-colorado-bitcoin-merchants) says the owner refused Bitcoin even though the cafe appeared on BTC Map. The exact branch should be matched before exclusion. Do not mark it verified based only on directory tags.
- **Butcher Block Cafe, 4605 East 74th Avenue**: the same article says 5002 Washington Street accepts Bitcoin but the family's other location does not. Its appendix nevertheless lists the second branch. This contradiction means the second branch must be held out of verified map additions until rechecked. The report also confirms Moonflower Coffee and the Washington Street Butcher Block location through actual purchases; those are independent firsthand corroboration, not merchant website claims.
