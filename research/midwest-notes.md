# Midwest merchant corroboration — October 20, 2026

States searched: Kansas, Nebraska, South Dakota, North Dakota, Minnesota, Iowa and Missouri. Searches covered statewide Bitcoin-acceptance terms, major-city cafe/restaurant/retail queries, dentistry and professional services, then merchant-specific follow-ups. This is a targeted web research pass, not an exhaustive census. No outreach or purchases. Root's BTC Map import is separate; OSM-derived sites are leads rather than independent corroboration.

The companion JSON contains 16 records: six `merchant-confirmed` through dated firsthand merchant accounts, eight `needs-review`, two `excluded`. Coordinates are supplied only for Maps Coffee & Chocolate from an exact sourced listing; others require geocoding or exact-source matching before a map pin is created. Unknown Bitcoin rails are `null`.

## Kansas

- **Maps Coffee & Chocolate**, Lenexa: [Bitcoin Party KC's directory](https://www.bitcoinpartykc.com/bitcoin-directory) identifies the cafe as the first shop the group onboarded through its 21 Merchant Initiative. The [merchant location page](https://mapscoffee.com/pages/maps-coffee-chocolate-location-lenexa) confirms the street address. Held as review evidence because the acceptance statement comes from the onboarding group, not the merchant's own site. It has BTC Map ID `node:13671942120`.
- **Touchstone Endodontics**, Lenexa: directory listing claims both rails; the merchant's [bill-pay page](https://www.touchstoneendo.com/pay-my-bill/) gives cards/CareCredit, and its [February 2025 financial policy](https://www.touchstoneendo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Touchstone-Endo-Consent-Forms-No-sign.pdf) lists cards, cash, checks and CareCredit. Neither independently confirms Bitcoin. Omission alone is not an explicit refusal, so this remains `needs-review` rather than `excluded`.
- Searches also covered Wichita, Fort Scott, Leawood and KC-area businesses. Many results were OSM derivatives or online restaurant gateways; these were not counted as fresh primary confirmations.

## Treatment of evidence

Merchant names and addresses are factual candidate data. Evidence summaries are paraphrased, and quote snippets are avoided. “Merchant-confirmed” means a dated merchant-owner statement or a clear firsthand onboarding/payment account was found; it does not mean this researcher personally performed a payment. Every `needs-review` reason is carried in JSON to prevent stale, indirect or location-ambiguous evidence from being upgraded during import.
