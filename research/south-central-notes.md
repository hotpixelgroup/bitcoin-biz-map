# South-central primary-source research

Checked October 20, 2026. Assigned states: Texas, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama. This is a targeted corroboration pass, not exhaustive coverage. National BTC Map collection is handled separately. No outreach occurred. Payment-rail `false` means the primary source did not establish that rail, not that the merchant rejects it. ## Strong national chain lead: Steak 'n Shake

[Merchant terms](https://www.steaknshake.com/terms-of-use/) last modified June 3, 2025, section 8 says: “You may elect to pay for certain in-store or drive-through orders using Bitcoin”. It explains QR payment via a compatible wallet/provider, final BTC payments, possible cash refunds and no merchant-added BTC fee. Terms alone do not explicitly name every branch.

[Official payment partner Speed's rollout announcement](https://www.tryspeed.com/blog/steak-n-shake-partners-with-speed), May 27, 2025, explicitly confirms Bitcoin Lightning implementation across all 300+ US locations, including counters, kiosks and drive-throughs. [Speed case study](https://www.tryspeed.com/steaknshake) states all 393 US locations accepted Bitcoin from May 16, 2025. Counts are point-in-time, not a current branch census. Root is assigning chain-location census separately; no duplicate branch JSON generated here.
